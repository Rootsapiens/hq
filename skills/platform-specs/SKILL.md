# Platform specs (CTO)

Stable formats:
- Vertical video and stories: 9:16, 1080x1920.
- Instagram feed portrait: 4:5, 1080x1350. Square: 1:1, 1080x1080.
- LinkedIn and X images: 1:1 or 1.91:1 landscape.

Platform limits (length, caption characters, hashtag counts, safe-zone margins) change often. Look them up from the platform's own help pages at the time of production, record the source and date in the asset file, and do not rely on memory.

## Checklist per asset
Ratio and resolution · length within the limit · text inside safe zones · captions present · file size within the limit · cover frame chosen.
