# Content calendar (CSO)

Save as `calendar-<client>-<period>.md` as a table:

| Date | Platform | Format | Pillar | Hook angle | Post id | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Rules
- Post ids are stable (`<client>-<yyyymmdd>-<n>`); every later file uses them.
- Balance: no pillar more than 40% of posts; mix formats (reels, carousels, stories, static).
- Put the strongest hook early in the week and around any launch date.
- Leave one flexible slot per week for trends or reactive content.
- Status starts at `brief` and moves through `create`, `produce`, `qa`, `approved`, `published` (the owner sets `published`).
