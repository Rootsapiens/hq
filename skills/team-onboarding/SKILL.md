# Onboarding (CPO)

## New team member — `onboarding-<name>.md`
- Role, reporting line, what "good" looks like in 90 days.
- Week 1: read docs/company.md, the brand kits of active clients, the QA checklist; shadow one pipeline end to end.
- 30/60/90: one measurable goal each.
- Access needed (the owner grants it; you only list it).

## New client — `onboarding-client-<client>.md`
- Contacts and approval chain (from the owner).
- Assets received: logo, fonts, photos, past posts, guidelines.
- Brand kit owner: Aktham. First brief: Zurub.
- Accounts and access the owner must request.
- Kick-off questions still open.
