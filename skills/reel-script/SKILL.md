# Reel script (CMO)

Save as `reel-<post id>.md`. Give **3 hook options**, then one full script.

## Structure (15 to 45 s)
| Time | Shot (what we see) | On-screen text | Voiceover / sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-2 s | Hook visual, movement in frame 1 | Hook line, max ~7 words | … |
| 2-… | Value beats, one idea each, a cut every 1.5 to 3 s | Short captions | … |
| last 3 s | Payoff + CTA | CTA | … |

## Rules
- The first frame must make sense with the sound off.
- One idea per reel. Cut anything that does not serve the hook's promise.
- Burned-in captions for all speech (accessibility).
- Keep text inside the safe zone (away from top, bottom and right-side UI); Aktham owns exact zones.
- Music: name a mood and tempo; Rajaie picks a licensed track. Never name a copyrighted song to use.
- End with one CTA that matches the brief's KPI.
