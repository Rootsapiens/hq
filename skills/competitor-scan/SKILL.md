# Competitor scan (CSO)

Save as `scan-<client>-<date>.md`.

For each account: name and link, platform, what they post (formats, pillars), 3 example posts with links, hook patterns, tone, visible engagement signals (only numbers you can see, with the date seen), what to borrow, what to avoid.

Finish with **5 opportunities** for the client, each tied to evidence above.

Only cite what you actually saw. If you cannot browse, say so and work only from material the owner provides.
