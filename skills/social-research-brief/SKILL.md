# Social research brief (CSO)

Save as `brief-<client>-<id>.md`. One page. Every claim about the market cites a source or is marked **unverified**.

## Template
- **Client / product:**
- **Goal:** one sentence (awareness, followers, leads, sales, event sign-ups).
- **KPI:** one primary metric and the target the owner gave. If no target was given, write "target: owner to set". Never invent one.
- **Audience:** who, where they are, what they care about, what stops their scroll. Mark assumptions.
- **Platforms and formats:** e.g. Instagram reel + carousel, TikTok video, LinkedIn post. Why each.
- **Content pillars:** 3 to 5 recurring themes.
- **Hook angles:** 5 options (problem, surprise, how-to, behind-the-scenes, social proof).
- **Brand rules:** voice, words to use and avoid, visual musts, legal or claim limits.
- **Competitors / references:** 2 to 3, with what to learn from each (sourced).
- **Cadence:** posts per week per platform.
- **Risks:** rights, claims, sensitive topics.

## Quality bar
A creator who has never met the client can make on-brief content from this page alone.
