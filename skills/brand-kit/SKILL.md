# Brand kit (CCO)

Save as `brandkit-<client>.md`.

- **Logo:** versions, clear space, minimum size, what never to do.
- **Colours:** primary, secondary, neutrals, accent, each as hex, with usage share and accessible text pairs.
- **Type:** heading and body fonts (with licence status), sizes for social, fallback.
- **Imagery:** photo style, illustration style, AI image style, what to avoid.
- **Voice:** 3 adjectives, words we use, words we never use, example lines.
- **Templates:** post, carousel, story, reel cover.

Only document what the client provided or the owner approved; mark proposals as **proposed**.
